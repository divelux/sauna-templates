House rules

A few simple rules keep the sauna clean, calm and friendly for everyone.

  1. Shower before the sauna.And again after the cold plunge, if you like.

  2. Sit on a towel.Every time, on every bench.

  3. Ask before you pour.Water on the stones means more steam for everyone in the room.

  4. Keep voices low.Phones stay in the lockers.

  5. Swimsuit or towel wrap.Please follow the sign for your session.

  6. No alcohol.Not before your session and not during it.

  7. Children with an adult.Guests under 16 come with a parent or guardian.

  8. Listen to your body.Leave the heat right away if you feel dizzy or unwell.

General guidance, not medical advice. If you are pregnant or have a heart condition or other health concern, talk to your doctor before using a sauna or a cold plunge.

Questions

What should I bring?

Two towels (one to sit on, one to dry off), a water bottle, flip-flops and a swimsuit. A wool felt sauna hat is welcome too. Wear it dry.

I have never been to a sauna. Can I come?

Yes. Start on a lower bench, where it is cooler, and keep your first rounds short. Our staff will show you the cold plunge and the rest area.

How cold is the plunge?

We post the water temperature at the front desk every day. Go in slowly, keep it short and get out if you feel unwell.

Can I book the whole sauna for a group?

Yes, on most evenings. Email us with the date, the time and how many people are coming.
